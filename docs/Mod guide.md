# Immersive Minecraft — Mod fieldbook

Pack 2026.10.13.3: 185 installed mod files, 153 in Vanilla protection. 171 additional bundled modules are listed separately.

These explanations describe mod capabilities. Settings, resource packs and server support determine which features appear. Full-profile terrain/gameplay additions do not change another server merely because they are on your client.

## Atmosphere

### Ambient Environment
Installed version: 26.2.1 · Both profiles · Client-side

**Short:** Adds subtle variation to the world's environmental colours.

**Medium:** Adjusts biome colour ambience so surroundings feel less visually uniform; it is a colour effect, not an ambient-sound mod.

**In depth:** Adjusts biome colour ambience so surroundings feel less visually uniform; it is a colour effect, not an ambient-sound mod. Grass, leaves and water can have small colour differences even within one biome. It is subtle colour variation rather than a replacement sky or new weather system. Included in Vanilla protection and the full profile.

[Author's project page](https://modrinth.com/mod/ambient-environment)

### Atmospheric Fauna
Installed version: 0.5.1 · Both profiles · Server support needed

**Short:** Adds decorative birds and other ambient wildlife.

**In depth:** Its client-side wildlife makes the landscape feel inhabited. These visual creatures are different from server-controlled mobs and do not imply new server loot or farming mechanics. The author describes particle-based birds with different behaviours in different biomes. These are decorative client fauna; other players need their own client setup to see equivalent effects.
